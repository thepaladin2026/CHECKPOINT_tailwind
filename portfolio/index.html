About me

I am a developer based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. Most of my work starts the same way: someone shows me a task they repeat every week, and I turn it into something that runs on its own.

I work across the whole chain, from the script that pulls the data to the interface the team actually opens. That means Python and Bash for the automation itself, Laravel or plain PHP for the business logic, and React when the result needs a real interface.

I care about tools people keep using after I hand them over, so I spend as much time on clear screens and readable code as on the automation behind them.
